# CoAssure — Go-to-Market Playbook

---

## Market Position: The Safety Intelligence Layer

CoAssure enters the market as **an assistant for the safety systems organisations already use** — not a replacement. This is a deliberate strategic choice.

Most organisations with more than 20 field workers have already invested in a safety platform (SafetyCulture, HammerTech, Procore, or others). They are not looking to rip and replace. But they are deeply frustrated by the quality of data going into those systems — forms filled on autopilot, generic SWMS signed without review, near-misses that never get reported.

CoAssure solves this by sitting alongside the existing platform and making the input better: richer conversations, context-aware prompts, voice-first interaction, and effortless reporting. The compliance system of record stays the same. The data flowing into it transforms.

**For mid-to-small sites that don't have a dedicated safety platform** — residential builders, small civil contractors, field service companies whose "system" is a filing cabinet — CoAssure becomes the primary tool. Same app, same experience, different backend role.

This dual positioning — **assistant on big sites, platform on small ones** — is the core of the go-to-market strategy.

## Competitive Landscape

### What Exists Today

| Competitor | What They Do | Where They Fall Short |

| **SafetyCulture (iAuditor)** | Digital inspections, checklists, audits. Large install base. Strong brand. | It's a digital form. Workers tick boxes on autopilot. | **viAct** | Computer vision for construction sites. Detects PPE violations, unsafe zones from video. | Hardware-dependent (cameras). Surveillance model — watches workers rather than engaging them. Opposite of CoAssure's philosophy. |
| **BuildPass** | AI voice-to-text transcription for toolbox meetings. Summary generation. | Transcribes and summarises, but doesn't facilitate. Doesn't ask questions, doesn't challenge, doesn't pull in context. Recording tool, not a thinking partner. |

### CoAssure's Differentiation

No existing tool does all five of these things:

1. **Starts with the worker's voice** — not a form, not a camera, not a dashboard
2. **Challenges the worker's thinking** — asks the question they hadn't considered, grounded in weather, location, org standards, and crowd-sourced data
3. **Helps fill out any form** — scan a QR, photograph a paper form, or select from a library. Works with the client's system, not against it
4. **Facilitates group discussions** — toolbox talks where the AI assists the supervisor or runs the briefing for small crews without one
5. **Works across organisational boundaries** — sub-contractors use it free on any client site. The output (with branded footer) demonstrates value to the next buyer

The positioning is not "better compliance software." It is: **"The voice-first safety assistant that makes every form better, every toolbox talk more useful, and every near-miss easier to report — on whatever system you already use."**

## The Buying Center

Safety software purchases in industrial organisations involve 7–20 stakeholders. Understanding who they are and what blocks or enables a deal is essential.

### Key Players

**The Initiator — Site Supervisor / HSE Advisor**

The person closest to the problem. They see the pencil-whipping daily. They run toolbox talks where nobody speaks. They chase paperwork that doesn't reflect reality.

- *How we reach them:* Product-led. They discover CoAssure because a subbie used it on their site, or because a peer recommended it.
- *What they need to see:* A worker using CoAssure to fill out a form in 2 minutes that normally takes 10 — and the output being richer.
- *Their blocker:* "Will my guys actually use it? Will it create more work for me?"

**The Influencer — IT Manager**

Cares about data security, integration, device management.

- *Our answer:* Cloud-hosted, standard APIs (SafetyCulture, Procore, HammerTech), no custom hardware, works on existing smartphones. SOC 2 compliance pathway.
- *Their blocker:* "Does it integrate with our existing stack? Where does the data go?"

**The Influencer — Union Representative**

Cares about worker privacy, surveillance, psychosocial impact. Especially relevant under the NSW Digital Work Systems Amendment (2025).

- *Our answer:* CoAssure is a worker empowerment tool. It does not monitor task speed, location history, or individual performance. Workers control their recordings. The tone is supportive, not punitive. No algorithmic management features.
- *Their blocker:* "Is this surveillance dressed up as safety?"

**The Influencer — Risk / Insurance Manager**

Cares about evidence for insurer negotiations and EMR optimisation.

- *Our answer:* Every session is timestamped, geo-tagged, weather-contextualised evidence of active risk assessment. Toolbox talk transcripts document genuine consultation. Near-miss data provides the leading indicators insurers increasingly want to see.
- *Their blocker:* "Does this data hold up in court? Is it admissible?"

**The Decider — Operations Director / CEO**

Cares about reducing incidents, demonstrating Zero Harm commitment, reducing admin overhead.

- *Our answer:* CoAssure gives live risk intelligence from the field, reduces admin time, provides the strongest possible evidence of due diligence, and the dashboard surfaces discoveries that traditional systems miss entirely.
- *Their blocker:* "What's the ROI? How fast do we see results?"

**The Gatekeeper — Procurement / Legal**

Cares about compliance, data sovereignty, contract terms.

- *Our answer:* Australian-hosted data. Compliant with the Privacy Act, the Evidence Act, and the new Digital Work Systems duty. No algorithmic management. Transparent system logic.
- *Their blocker:* "Does this create legal exposure? What happens to the voice data?"

## Go-to-Market Motions

### Motion 1: Product-Led Growth (Bottom-Up via Sub-Contractors)

**The free tier is the growth engine.** Individual workers and sub-contractors adopt CoAssure because it's the fastest way to fill out a safety form.

**The funnel:**

```
Sub-contractor downloads free app
        ↓
Uses it to fill out client's form (scan/photo/voice)
        ↓
Pastes completed form into client's system
        ↓
Output includes branded footer + context link
        ↓
Client safety team sees the link, clicks it
        ↓
Sees the depth of the assessment (weather, GPS,
transcript, AI prompts)
        ↓
Asks "what is this?" → visits coassure.com
        ↓
Trials with own team → upgrades to paid tier
```

**Key metric:** Number of unique organisations that receive a CoAssure-linked submission from a free-tier user. This is the top of the enterprise funnel — and it costs nothing to generate.

**Why this works:**
- Zero acquisition cost for the initial user
- The product sells itself through the quality of the output
- The client sees the value before we ever make contact
- Sub-contractors spread it across multiple client sites simultaneously

### Motion 2: Sales-Led (Top-Down via Pilot)

For organisations that discover CoAssure through the product-led funnel, through industry events, or through direct outreach.

**The sales process:**

1. **Discovery call.** Understand their current safety workflow. Ask: "What percentage of your Take 5s contain meaningful, site-specific hazard information?" (The answer is almost always under 10%.) Ask: "How many near-misses were reported last month?" (The answer is almost always "not enough.")

2. **Pilot proposal.** 1 site, 1 team, 4 weeks. Free. No integration required. Workers download the app and use it alongside their existing process. Include facilitated toolbox talks if the site has team briefings.

3. **Pilot review.** Show the data:
   - Compare the depth of CoAssure conversations against existing Take 5 submissions from the same period
   - Show the near-miss and hazard reports that were captured (that wouldn't have been otherwise)
   - Show the toolbox talk transcripts vs. the old sign-in sheets
   - Show any Discovery Insights the AI identified during the pilot

4. **Enterprise proposal.** Roll out across all sites. Add org standards, dashboard, integrations.

**The pilot is the sales pitch.** We don't need slide decks. We need 4 weeks of real data showing the difference.

### Motion 3: The Sub-Contractor Mandate (Viral Expansion)

Once an enterprise customer adopts CoAssure, a secondary adoption wave follows:

- The enterprise client sees the value of CoAssure-linked submissions from sub-contractors
- They encourage (or require) visiting subbies to use the free tier or the Guest Pass
- Those sub-contractors start using it on other client sites
- Those other clients see the CoAssure links
- The cycle repeats

**This is how CoAssure becomes an industry norm.** Not through top-down mandates, but through bottom-up demonstration.

## Pricing Strategy

### Why Not Per-Seat

Per-seat pricing fails in field services:
- **High turnover.** Construction and trades have 30%+ annual turnover. Paying for seats that change hands is painful.
- **Variable headcount.** Project-based companies scale up and down. Fixed seat counts don't match.
- **Low utilisation.** In many enterprise SaaS deployments, 40–70% of paid seats are actively used. The effective cost per active user is far higher than listed.

### The CoAssure Pricing Model

| Tier | Pricing Basis | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 / forever | Voice pre-start, form assistant (scan/photo/fill), weather + location context, paste button, PDF export. Branded footer on all outputs. |
| **Team** | Per active team / month | Everything in Free + AI challenge questions, facilitated toolbox talks, easy reporting (near-miss/hazard/observation), post-job reflection, crowd-sourced context. No branded footer. |
| **Enterprise** | Per site or per project / month | Everything in Team + standards library, manager dashboard, rules engine, discovery insights, integrations, sub-contractor visibility, audit trail, weekly digest. |
| **Platform** | Per organisation / month | Everything in Enterprise + full platform mode (primary safety system for mid-to-small sites), multi-language, cross-project intelligence. |

**Key principles:**
- The free tier is genuinely useful on its own — not crippled. The branded footer is the only trade-off, and it's also the growth engine.
- Team pricing is per active team, not per seat. Add or remove workers without changing the price.
- Enterprise pricing is per site or project. Unlimited users within the boundary.
- Platform pricing is for organisations using CoAssure as their primary safety system.

### The ROI Case

**1. Time savings (immediate, measurable)**
- Voice is 75–90% faster than manual form entry
- A worker doing 3 pre-starts per day saves 20–30 minutes daily
- For a 50-person team, that's 80–125 hours per week returned to productive work
- At a loaded labour rate of $80/hour: $6,400–$10,000 per week in recaptured productivity

**2. Insurance premium reduction (medium-term)**
- Insurers offer premium discounts (up to 15–20%) for companies demonstrating proactive risk management with technology
- For a company paying $500K/year in workers' comp premiums, a 10% reduction is $50K/year
- CoAssure provides the evidence: timestamped, contextualised safety conversations before every task

**3. Incident cost avoidance (long-term, transformative)**
- Average cost of a serious workplace injury in Australia: $250K–$500K (direct + indirect)
- For every $1 in direct injury cost, $2–$3 in indirect costs (downtime, investigation, legal, lost contracts)
- A co-pilot that prevents even one serious incident pays for itself across the enterprise for years

**4. Near-miss intelligence (new value)**
- Near-miss data is the leading indicator that predicts serious incidents
- Most organisations capture a fraction of near-misses because reporting friction is too high
- CoAssure's voice reporting unlocks 5–10x more near-miss data — each one a signal that can prevent a future injury

## The Assistant-to-Platform Path

### Phase 1: Assistant for Existing Systems

CoAssure integrates with the platforms organisations already use:

- **SafetyCulture:** Push completed inspections and reports via SafetyCulture's REST API. Pull template structures. Require Premium or Enterprise plan for API access.
- **Procore:** Push completed safety records to project documents via Procore's REST API (OAuth 2.0). Pull project and vendor data.
- **HammerTech:** Sync worker records and push completed forms. HammerTech's Procore integration provides a bridge path.
- **SharePoint / Google Drive:** Export PDFs and structured records to document management systems.
- **Email:** Auto-send completed forms and reports to specified recipients.
- **Paste-in:** For any platform without API integration, generate structured text for clipboard paste into any free-text field.

The assistant model works because it doesn't threaten the existing investment. The compliance platform stays. The data flowing into it gets dramatically better.

### Phase 2: Platform for Mid-to-Small Sites

For organisations where the existing "system" is paper, spreadsheets, or a shared drive — CoAssure becomes the primary safety management tool:

- Pre-start assessments (voice-first)
- Facilitated toolbox talks (with full transcripts)
- Hazard and near-miss reporting (tap, talk, photo)
- SWMS and JSA creation (conversational, with form scanning)
- Form library and template management
- Team dashboard with discovery insights
- Audit trail and compliance records

**This is the natural upgrade path.** An organisation starts using CoAssure as an assistant alongside their ad-hoc safety process. They realise the CoAssure dashboard gives them better visibility than their spreadsheet. The facilitated toolbox talks produce better records than the sign-in sheets. The near-miss reports provide data they never had. They stop maintaining the parallel system.

**Target sites for platform mode:**
- Residential construction (builders, renovators)
- Small-to-medium commercial projects
- Field service companies (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, telecoms)
- Maintenance and facilities management
- Agricultural and regional operations

These sites have the same compliance obligations as Tier 1 projects but a fraction of the safety infrastructure. CoAssure gives them enterprise-grade safety intelligence at a fraction of the cost and complexity.

## The First 12 Months

### Months 1–3: Build and Validate (Free Tier)

- Ship the free-tier app (Stage 1: Voice Assistant)
- Core features: voice pre-start, form scanning (QR + photo), weather/location context, paste button, PDF export, branded footer
- Target 200 individual workers / sole traders / small crews through trade forums, social media, word of mouth
- Measure: Are people using it voluntarily? Is the paste button being used? Are branded links being clicked by client safety teams?

### Months 4–6: Add Intelligence and Teams (Paid Tier)

- Ship Stage 2 (Safety Partner: AI challenges, facilitated toolbox talks, easy reporting)
- Launch Team tier pricing
- Target 10 small-to-medium companies for paid pilots
- Measure: Do workers report the AI asked them something useful? Are near-miss reports increasing? Are toolbox talk transcripts richer?

### Months 7–9: Enterprise and Standards

- Ship Stage 3 (Org Brain: standards library, dashboard, discovery insights, integrations)
- Launch Enterprise tier
- Target 3 enterprise pilots (mid-size contractors, utilities, or facilities management companies)
- Measure: Are managers getting discoveries from the dashboard they couldn't get before? Are integrations with SafetyCulture/Procore working smoothly?

### Months 10–12: Platform and Ecosystem

- Ship Stage 4 (Platform mode for mid-to-small sites, multi-language)
- Activate the viral loop: enterprise clients encourage subbies to use CoAssure
- Launch Platform tier for organisations using CoAssure as their primary system
- Measure: How many new organisations are receiving CoAssure-linked submissions? What's the conversion rate from "link click" to "trial"? How many organisations have switched from assistant to platform mode?

## Competitive Moat

Over time, CoAssure builds defensibility through:

1. **Network data.** Every conversation adds to the crowd-sourced hazard intelligence. The more workers use it at a location, the smarter it gets about that location. Competitors starting later have less data.

2. **Organisational lock-in.** Once a company uploads their standards and configures their rules engine, switching costs are real. The AI has learned their procedures, their priorities, their language.

3. **Cross-organisational presence.** When sub-contractors use CoAssure across multiple client sites, the system sees hazard patterns that no single-organisation tool can see. This intelligence becomes a unique selling point for enterprise customers.

4. **Worker brand trust.** If workers adopt CoAssure voluntarily (free tier) because it genuinely helps them, that's a moat no enterprise sales motion can replicate. The tool the worker chooses beats the tool the company mandates.

5. **Form intelligence.** As CoAssure scans and processes thousands of different safety forms across hundreds of organisations, it builds an unmatched understanding of how safety documentation works in practice — which forms are common, which fields matter, which sections are always left blank, and how to help workers complete them better.

# CoAssure — Go-to-Market Playbook

---

## The Market We Are Entering

The industrial safety software market is large, fragmented, and ripe for disruption — not because the technology is missing, but because the existing tools solve the wrong problem. They digitise paperwork. They do not change thinking.

CoAssure enters through a specific gap: **the remote/lone field worker who has no safety support between the depot and the job site.** This is not a crowded segment. Most competitors target large construction sites with supervisors on the ground. We target the worker in the van.

## Competitive Landscape

### What Exists Today

| Competitor | What They Do | Their Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **SafetyCulture (iAuditor)** | Digital inspections, checklists, and audits. Strong brand. Large install base. | It is a digital form. Workers still tick boxes. No conversational engagement, no contextual intelligence. |
| **HammerTech** | Compliance management platform for construction. Permit-to-work, inductions. | Enterprise-focused. Designed for large sites with supervisors. Doesn't help the lone worker in the van. |
| **Procore** | Project management with safety modules. Industry standard for Tier 1 builders. | Safety is a module, not the product. Forms-based. Subbies interact with it reluctantly. |
| **Field1st** | Voice-first JSA and inspection tool. AI hazard detection from photos. | Closest to our space. But designed for supervisors documenting inspections, not workers thinking out loud. |
| **aiOla** | Speech-to-workflow for operations. High-accuracy ASR in noisy environments. | Focused on manufacturing and logistics workflows, not field safety conversations. No organisational safety intelligence layer. |
| **viAct** | Computer vision AI for construction sites. Detects PPE violations, unsafe zones. | Hardware-dependent (cameras). Surveillance model. Does not engage with the worker — watches them. Opposite of our philosophy. |
| **Fresco AI (Procore)** | AI co-pilot for project documentation. Natural language queries on project data. | Designed for project managers querying documents, not field workers doing pre-starts. |

### Where CoAssure Fits

None of these tools do what CoAssure does:

1. **Start with the worker's voice** — not a form, not a camera, not a dashboard query
2. **Challenge the worker's thinking** — ask the question they haven't considered
3. **Pull in live context automatically** — weather, location, org standards, crowd-sourced hazards
4. **Generate compliance as a byproduct** — the documentation comes from the conversation, not instead of it
5. **Work across organisational boundaries** — subbies can use it on any client site without the client buying anything

Our positioning is not "better compliance software." It is: **"The 30-second conversation that replaces the 10-minute form — and actually makes people safer."**

## The Buying Center

Industrial safety purchasing decisions are not made by one person. A typical deal involves 7–20 stakeholders. Understanding who they are and what they care about is essential.

### The Key Players

**The Initiator — Site Supervisor / HSE Advisor**
- *Who they are:* The person closest to the problem. They see the pencil-whipping every day.
- *What they want:* Workers who actually think before they start. Less paperwork chasing. Faster pre-starts.
- *How we reach them:* Product-led. They discover CoAssure because a subbie used it on their site, or because a peer recommended it. They trial it with their team.
- *Their concern:* "Will my guys actually use it?"

**The Influencer — IT Manager**
- *What they care about:* Data security, integration with existing platforms, device management.
- *Our answer:* Cloud-hosted with SOC 2 compliance pathway. Standard API integrations. No custom hardware required — works on the phones workers already carry.

**The Influencer — Union Representative**
- *What they care about:* Worker privacy, surveillance risk, psychosocial impact (under new NSW Digital Work Systems laws).
- *Our answer:* CoAssure is a worker empowerment tool, not a tracking tool. It does not monitor task speed, location history, or individual performance. Workers control their own recordings. The tone is supportive, not punitive.

**The Influencer — Risk / Insurance Manager**
- *What they care about:* Evidence of proactive risk management for insurer negotiations. EMR optimisation.
- *Our answer:* Every CoAssure session is a timestamped, geo-tagged, weather-contextualised record of active risk assessment. This is the evidence insurers want when setting premiums.

**The Decider — Operations Director / CEO**
- *What they care about:* Reducing incident rates. Demonstrating "Zero Harm" commitment. Reducing admin overhead. Operational efficiency.
- *Our answer:* CoAssure gives you live risk intelligence from the field, reduces admin time by 30–60 minutes per worker per day, and provides the strongest possible evidence of due diligence.

**The Gatekeeper — Procurement / Legal**
- *What they care about:* Compliance with the WHS Act, the new NSW Digital Work Systems Amendment, data sovereignty, contract terms.
- *Our answer:* Australian-hosted data. Compliant with the Privacy Act, the Evidence Act, and the new Digital Work Systems duty. No algorithmic management features. Transparent system logic.

## Go-to-Market Motions

### Motion 1: Product-Led Growth (Bottom-Up)

**The free tier is the growth engine.**

Individual workers and small crews adopt CoAssure because it's faster than typing. They use it on client sites. The branded footer on their outputs creates awareness with the client's safety team.

**The funnel:**

```
Individual worker downloads free app
        ↓
Uses it to fill out client forms faster
        ↓
Client safety team sees CoAssure links in submissions
        ↓
Clicks link → sees rich context page
        ↓
Asks "what is this?" → visits coassure.com
        ↓
Trials it with their own team
        ↓
Upgrades to Team or Enterprise tier
```

**Key metric:** Number of unique organisations that receive a CoAssure-linked submission from a free-tier user. This is the top of the enterprise funnel.

**Why this works:**
- Zero acquisition cost for the initial user — they adopt it for personal utility
- The product sells itself to the next buyer through the quality of the output
- The client sees the value before we ever make contact

### Motion 2: Sales-Led (Top-Down)

For organisations that discover CoAssure through the product-led funnel or through direct outreach.

**The sales process:**

1. **Discovery call:** Understand their current safety workflow. Ask: "What percentage of your Take 5s contain meaningful, site-specific hazard information?" (The answer is almost always under 10%.)
2. **Pilot proposal:** 1 site, 1 team, 4 weeks. Free. No integration required. Workers download the app and use it alongside their existing process.
3. **Pilot review:** Show them the data. Compare the depth of CoAssure conversations against their existing Take 5 submissions from the same period.
4. **Enterprise proposal:** Roll out across all sites. Add org standards, dashboard, integrations.

**The pilot is the sales pitch.** We don't need slide decks. We need 4 weeks of real data showing the difference between "All good" and an actual safety conversation.

### Motion 3: The Sub-Contractor Mandate (Viral Expansion)

Once an enterprise customer adopts CoAssure, we create a secondary adoption wave:

- The enterprise client can require visiting sub-contractors to use the **Guest Pass** (QR code check-in) before starting work
- Sub-contractors who use it on this site start using it on other client sites (free tier)
- Those other clients see the CoAssure links in submissions
- The cycle repeats

**This is how CoAssure becomes an industry standard, not just a product.**

## Pricing Strategy

### Why Not Per-Seat

Per-seat pricing fails in field services:
- **High turnover.** Construction and trades have 30%+ annual turnover. Paying for seats that change hands constantly is painful for procurement.
- **Variable headcount.** Project-based companies scale up and down. Fixed seat counts don't match.
- **Low utilisation.** In many enterprise SaaS deployments, only 40–70% of paid seats are actively used. The effective cost per active user is far higher than the listed price.

### The CoAssure Pricing Model

| Tier | Pricing Basis | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 / forever | Voice pre-start, transcription, weather + location context, paste button, branded footer on outputs |
| **Team** | Per active team / month | Everything in Free + AI challenge questions, toolbox broadcasts, hazard voice notes, reflect, crowd-sourced context. No branded footer on outputs. |
| **Enterprise** | Per site or per project / month | Everything in Team + standards library, manager dashboard, rules engine, project context, sub-contractor Guest Pass, integrations, audit trail, weekly digest. |

**Key pricing principles:**
- **The free tier has no time limit and no feature cripple that makes it useless.** It must be genuinely valuable on its own. The branded footer is the only trade-off — and it's also the growth engine.
- **Team pricing is per active team, not per seat.** A "team" is a group of workers under one manager. Add or remove individuals without changing the price.
- **Enterprise pricing is per site or per project.** Unlimited users within the boundary. This aligns with how construction and field services think about cost — by project, not by headcount.

### The ROI Case

The pricing is justified by three value streams:

**1. Time savings (immediate, measurable)**
- Voice is 75–90% faster than manual form entry
- A worker doing 3 pre-starts per day saves 20–30 minutes daily
- For a 50-person team, that's 80–125 hours per week returned to productive work
- At a loaded labour rate of $80/hour, that's $6,400–$10,000 per week in recaptured productivity

**2. Insurance premium reduction (medium-term, significant)**
- Insurers offer premium discounts (up to 15–20%) for companies that demonstrate proactive risk management with technology
- For a company paying $500K/year in workers' compensation premiums, a 10% reduction is $50K/year
- CoAssure provides the evidence: timestamped, contextualised safety conversations before every task

**3. Incident cost avoidance (long-term, transformative)**
- The average cost of a serious workplace injury in Australia is $250K–$500K (direct + indirect costs)
- For every $1 in direct injury cost, there is $2–$3 in indirect costs (downtime, investigation, retraining, legal, lost contracts)
- A co-pilot that prevents even one serious incident pays for itself across the entire enterprise for years

## Channel Strategy

For Enterprise deals. Triggered by:
- Product-led funnel (client safety team clicks a CoAssure link from a subbie submission)
- Inbound from conference/event presence
- Referral from existing customers

### Partnerships

**Safety consultancies:** Firms that advise companies on WHS compliance. CoAssure becomes part of their recommended technology stack. They introduce us to their clients.

**Insurance brokers:** Brokers looking for ways to help clients reduce premiums. CoAssure provides the data insurers want. Broker introduces us to their book of business.

**Industry associations:** Master Builders, Housing Industry Association, Plumbing associations, electrical trade bodies. Co-branded content, conference sponsorship, member discounts.

**Platform integrations:** Build lightweight integrations with Procore, SafetyCulture, HammerTech, and ServiceM8. Not to replace them — to feed better data into them. Every integration makes CoAssure stickier and easier to adopt.

## The First 12 Months

### Months 1–3: Build and Validate

- Ship the free-tier app (Stage 1: Smart Voice Note)
- Target 200 individual workers / sole traders through trade forums, social media, word of mouth
- Measure: Are people using it voluntarily? Is the paste button being used? Are branded links being clicked?

### Months 4–6: Add Intelligence and Teams

- Ship Stage 2 (Active Listener: AI challenge, toolbox broadcasts, hazard notes)
- Launch Team tier pricing
- Target 10 small-to-medium field service companies for paid pilots
- Measure: Do workers report the AI asked them something useful? Are managers using the toolbox broadcast?

### Months 7–9: Enterprise and Standards

- Ship Stage 3 (Org Brain: standards library, dashboard, integrations)
- Launch Enterprise tier
- Target 3 enterprise pilots (mid-size contractors or utilities)
- Measure: Are managers getting value from the dashboard? Are org standards being woven into conversations effectively?

### Months 10–12: The Ecosystem

- Ship the Guest Pass (QR code for sub-contractors)
- Activate the viral loop: enterprise clients require subbies to check in via Guest Pass
- Subbies discover CoAssure → use free tier on other sites → other clients discover CoAssure
- Measure: How many new organisations are receiving CoAssure-linked submissions? What's the conversion rate from "link click" to "trial"?

## Competitive Moat

Over time, CoAssure builds defensibility through:

1. **Network data.** Every conversation adds to the crowd-sourced hazard intelligence. The more workers use it, the smarter it gets about specific locations, conditions, and trades. Competitors starting later have less data.

2. **Organisational lock-in.** Once a company uploads their standards and configures their rules engine, switching costs are real. The AI has learned their procedures, their priorities, their language.

3. **Cross-organisational presence.** When subbies use CoAssure across multiple client sites, the system sees hazard patterns that no single-organisation tool can. This intelligence becomes a unique selling point for enterprise customers.

4. **Brand trust with workers.** If workers adopt CoAssure voluntarily (free tier) because it genuinely helps them, that's a moat no enterprise sales motion can replicate. The tool the worker chooses beats the tool the company mandates.
